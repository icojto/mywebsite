## Recommendations

- Keep card/preview images around 150 KB to 500 KB where possible.
- Keep popup still images around 300 KB to 1.5 MB where possible.
- Convert large GIFs to MP4/WebM for website use.
- Very large PNG screenshots should be exported as WebP or optimized JPG unless transparency is required.
- If a motion asset is above roughly 10–15 MB, strongly consider conversion to MP4.
